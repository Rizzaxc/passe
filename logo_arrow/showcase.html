Passe · Logo Concepts

Six directions inspired by the red→pink motion-arrow mark · slogan “Play Together. Win Together”
01 · Double Blade
Forward chevrons
The closest heir to the reference: two stacked blades reading “»” — momentum, the pass moving downfield.
02 · Monogram
“P” with a dart
The brand initial as a solid glyph, with a pink dart notched into the bowl — an app-icon-ready mark.
03 · Pass Arc
Ball-to-teammate
One player (dot) sends a pass along a trajectory to another (arrowhead). Literal “play together”.
04 · Victory
Two rise to an apex
Two blades converge into an upward peak — a “V” and a rising chart. Two teams, one win.
05 · Flash
Speed bolt
A single lightning-dart carrying the full gradient sweep — the most abstract, energetic, sticker-friendly mark.
06 · Roundel
Community loop
An open ring turning into an arrow — motion that keeps coming back. Reads as a community that plays on repeat.
